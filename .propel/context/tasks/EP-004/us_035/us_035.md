# User Story - US_035

## Story ID
* ID: US_035

## Story Title
* Staff-Assisted Patient Booking

## Description
* As a staff member, I want to create appointment bookings on behalf of patients without requiring patient-side verification steps so that I can efficiently book appointments for patients who call or visit in person.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Given** I am authenticated as a staff member, **When** I search for a patient by name or phone number and select a slot on their behalf, **Then** a booking is created for the patient without triggering patient-side verification requirements.
2. **Given** I complete a staff-assisted booking, **When** the booking is confirmed, **Then** the patient receives the standard confirmation email and ICS artifacts, and the booking is attributed to the staff member who created it.
3. **Given** the patient does not yet have an account, **When** I create a staff-assisted booking, **Then** I can create a basic patient profile inline and attach the booking to the new profile.
4. **Given** the staff-assisted booking is created, **When** I review the booking record, **Then** the audit log shows the booking was created by a staff actor on behalf of the patient.

## Edge Cases
* What happens if the patient already has a conflicting appointment at the same time? (System shows a conflict warning with the existing appointment details; staff can proceed with an override acknowledgment.)
* How does the system handle a staff member trying to book for themselves? (Staff can book as a patient using the patient portal; staff-assisted booking is not for self-booking.)
