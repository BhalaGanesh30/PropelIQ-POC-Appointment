# User Story - US_046

## Story ID
* ID: US_046

## Story Title
* Drug-Drug and Drug-Allergy Conflict Detection

## Description
* As a clinician, I want the system to detect drug-drug and drug-allergy conflicts with severity classification and require my acknowledgment for critical alerts so that patient safety is protected during clinical review.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Given** a patient profile contains medications and allergies, **When** the conflict detection engine evaluates the clinical facts, **Then** drug-drug and drug-allergy conflicts are identified and classified by severity (Low, Moderate, High, Critical).
2. **Given** a conflict is detected, **When** the patient profile is displayed, **Then** conflict alert banners appear above the medications section with the severity label and a description of the conflict.
3. **Given** a Critical severity conflict is identified, **When** the clinician views the profile, **Then** a mandatory acknowledgment dialog is shown and the clinician cannot dismiss the profile without explicitly acknowledging the critical alert.
4. **Given** the clinician acknowledges a critical conflict, **When** the acknowledgment is recorded, **Then** the acknowledgment is logged in the audit trail with the clinician's identity and timestamp.

## Edge Cases
* What happens if the conflict detection rules database is outdated? (Conflict engine continues with available rules; a staleness warning is shown; a background update process refreshes the rules.)
* How does the system handle a patient with 20+ medications presenting many conflict pairs? (Conflicts are deduplicated; only unique pairs are shown; the highest severity per drug pair is displayed to avoid alert fatigue.)
