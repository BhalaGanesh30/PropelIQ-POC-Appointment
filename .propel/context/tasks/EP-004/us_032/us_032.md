# User Story - US_032

## Story ID
* ID: US_032

## Story Title
* Staff Arrival Check-In Workflow

## Description
* As a staff member, I want to perform one-click patient arrival check-in and transition appointment states so that the queue accurately reflects patient presence in the clinic.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Given** a patient arrives and I locate their appointment on the queue dashboard, **When** I click "Check In," **Then** the appointment status transitions from "Scheduled" to "Arrived" and the timestamp is recorded.
2. **Given** a patient is checked in, **When** I click "Start Visit," **Then** the appointment status transitions to "In-Progress" and the visit start time is recorded.
3. **Given** a visit is in progress, **When** I click "Complete Visit," **Then** the appointment status transitions to "Completed" and the visit end time is recorded.
4. **Given** a patient does not arrive, **When** I mark them as "No-Show," **Then** the appointment status transitions to "No-Show" and the audit log records the staff member who performed the action.

## Edge Cases
* What happens if a state transition is performed out of order (e.g., completing a visit before starting it)? (State machine validation rejects the invalid transition with a descriptive error message.)
* How does the system handle check-in for a patient not yet in the queue? (Walk-in creation flow is triggered automatically; see US_033.)
