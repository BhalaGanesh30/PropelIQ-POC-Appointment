# User Story - US_018

## Story ID
* ID: US_018

## Story Title
* Password Reset and Account Lockout

## Description
* As a registered user, I want to reset my password via email and have my account automatically locked after 5 failed login attempts so that I can regain secure access and the platform is protected from brute-force attacks.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Given** I am on the login page and click "Forgot Password", **When** I enter my registered email address, **Then** a password reset link is sent to my email within 2 minutes and the system does not confirm whether the email exists (security: same response for registered and unregistered emails).
2. **Given** I receive the password reset email, **When** I click the link and submit a new password meeting complexity requirements (8+ characters, 1 uppercase, 1 number, 1 special character), **Then** my password is updated, the reset link is invalidated, and I am redirected to the login page.
3. **Given** I enter an incorrect password 5 times consecutively, **When** the 5th failed attempt is recorded, **Then** my account is locked for 30 minutes, all active sessions are invalidated, and I receive an email notification of the lockout.
4. **Given** my account is locked, **When** 30 minutes elapse, **Then** the account unlocks automatically and I can attempt login again.

## Edge Cases
* What happens if I click a password reset link more than 24 hours after it was issued? (Link is expired; system displays "Reset link expired" with an option to request a new one.)
* How does the system handle multiple password reset requests within a short period? (Rate limiting: maximum 3 reset requests per 15 minutes per account to prevent email flooding.)
