# User Story - US_023

## Story ID
* ID: US_023

## Story Title
* Preferred-Slot Waitlist and Claim Window

## Description
* As a patient, I want to join a preferred-slot waitlist so that I am notified when my desired appointment type becomes available and I have 2 hours to claim it before it is offered to the next eligible patient.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Given** no slots match my search criteria, **When** I click "Join Waitlist," **Then** my preferred slot parameters (date range, duration, type) are saved and I receive a confirmation that I am on the waitlist.
2. **Given** a matching slot becomes available (due to cancellation or release), **When** the system identifies eligible waitlisted patients, **Then** the first eligible patient receives a preferred-slot alert notification within 5 minutes of the slot becoming available.
3. **Given** I receive a preferred-slot alert, **When** I claim the slot within 2 hours, **Then** the slot is reserved for me and I receive the standard booking confirmation artifacts.
4. **Given** I do not claim the slot within 2 hours, **When** the claim window expires, **Then** the slot is released and the next eligible waitlisted patient is notified.

## Edge Cases
* What happens if two waitlisted patients try to claim the same slot simultaneously? (Only the first claim succeeds using atomic reservation; the second claimant is notified the slot was taken and remains on the waitlist.)
* How does the system handle patients who are away during the claim window? (Patients can configure claim-window preferences; unclaimed slots automatically proceed to the next eligible patient.)
