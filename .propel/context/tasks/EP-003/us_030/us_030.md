# User Story - US_030

## Story ID
* ID: US_030

## Story Title
* Preferred-Slot Alert with Claim Countdown

## Description
* As a waitlisted patient, I want to receive an immediate preferred-slot alert notification with a visible claim countdown so that I can act quickly to claim my desired appointment before it is offered to someone else.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Given** I am on the preferred-slot waitlist and a matching slot becomes available, **When** the system dispatches the alert, **Then** I receive an email and/or SMS (per my channel preferences) within 5 minutes of the slot opening.
2. **Given** I receive the preferred-slot alert, **When** I open the claim link, **Then** I see the slot details and a visible countdown timer showing remaining time (2 hours) with urgency color shift at 30 minutes remaining.
3. **Given** I view the claim page, **When** I click "Claim Appointment," **Then** the slot is reserved for me atomically and I receive the standard booking confirmation artifacts.
4. **Given** the 2-hour claim window expires, **When** I attempt to claim after expiry, **Then** the link is invalidated and I am informed the slot was offered to another patient.

## Edge Cases
* What happens if the patient claims the slot but the confirmation email fails? (Slot is still reserved; retry logic attempts email redelivery; patient can access confirmation from their dashboard.)
* How does the system handle timezone differences for the claim countdown? (Countdown is always shown in the patient's browser timezone; the expiry timestamp is stored in UTC and converted client-side.)
